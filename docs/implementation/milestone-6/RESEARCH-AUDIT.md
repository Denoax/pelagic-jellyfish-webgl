## Sources actually reviewed

- NOAA [vent factsheet](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/edu/materials/hydrothermal-vents-fact-sheet.pdf): mineral precipitation, not burning material. [Small smoker](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/multimedia/okeanos-explorations-22voyage-to-the-ridge-gallery-media-ex2205-dive02-smoker/) video downloaded and sampled: broken accretions, dark outlets, localized pale/rust crust, small fauna. ROV illumination is not natural ambient lighting.
- NOAA [pillow lava](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/multimedia/edu-themes-vents-and-volcanoes-media-multimedia-pillow/) image inspected: overlapping stretched lobes with crust fractures rather than unrelated random rocks. [ASHES venting](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/multimedia/ashes-venting/) identifies local white mineral/microbial patches; [PMEL FAQ](https://www.pmel.noaa.gov/eoi/FAQ.html) distinguishes clear diffuse shimmer from focused mineral-laden discharge. [ASHES site](https://www.pmel.noaa.gov/eoi/nemo2001/explorer/ashes.html) documents concentrated chimneys and diffuse communities, not a uniform carpet.
- NOAA [Meet the Deep](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/exploration-extras/24-national-ocean-month/), [marine snow](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/ocean-fact/marinesnow/), [light/color](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/edu/materials/light-and-color-fact-sheet.pdf), [bioluminescence](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/ocean-fact/bioluminescence/): preserve settling organic material and dark water; warm pigments need local illumination.
- Schmidt [Tracking Down Vents](https://schmidtocean.org/cruise-log-post/tracking-down-hydrothermal-vents/), [Wow to Why](https://schmidtocean.org/cruise-log-post/hydrothermal-hunt-wow/), [A Look into Chimneys](https://schmidtocean.org/cruise-log-post/look-chimneys-insights-hydrothermal-hunt/), [Virtual Vents](https://schmidtocean.org/cruise/virtual-vents-changing-face-hydrothermalism-revealed/): source buoyancy, widening/dilution then lateral current; large irregular architecture; a connected field rather than one isolated prop. Text/source review, not a claim of watching all expedition videos.
- MBARI [bioluminescence research](https://www.mbari.org/project/bioluminescence-and-fluorescence/), [Glowing to the Bottom](https://www.mbari.org/news/glowing-to-the-bottom/), [2019 research](https://annualreport.mbari.org/2019/story/bioluminescence-behavior-biochemistry-and-genomics): biological light is selective, not blanket glowing geology. Reference only, no copied assets.
- WHOI [Lost City research](https://www.whoi.edu/fileserver.do?id=31963&p=15966&pt=2): up-to-60 m carbonate structures are a scale reference only. This sanctuary remains a basalt-hosted sulfide interpretation, not white alkaline carbonate architecture.

- Unknown Worlds [Lava Zone concept development](https://unknownworlds.com/en/news/subnautica-concept-art-lava-zone-2-2): adapt authored large geological masses and exploration-readable silhouettes, reject lava, fiery color and game-biome clutter.
