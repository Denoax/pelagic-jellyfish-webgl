# Animal-quality constraints

Mani's September 7 Milestone 1 continuation requires geometry and tissue quality,
not additional scene features. Preserve the existing renderer, simulation,
activation contract and opt-in development preview; do not change default animals.

- Rim defects must be diagnosed at their source, not hidden with bloom.
- Oral arms remain rich folded membranes with connected roots and coherent lag.
- Judge living tissue through thickness, transmission/scattering approximation
  and soft moving highlights, not global brightness, opacity or saturation.
- Compare rest, contraction, recovery, side, underside, oblique and near/far views.
- Record actual motion and uncaptured performance separately before review.
- A visual review gate is not passed solely by tests or reduced defect severity.
