# Bundled 3D reference assets

- `smithsonian-pocillopora.glb` — *Pocillopora damicornis caespitosa*, Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History / Smithsonian Open Access, CC0. Source package: `3d_package:14eb9321-2308-4878-8adb-831d078ba0b0`. Downloaded from the Smithsonian 3D API's `Low_Resolution` GLB derivative for use as a real coral specimen in the reef environment.
- `smithsonian-diploria.glb` — *Diploria labyrinthiformis*, Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History / Smithsonian Open Access, CC0. Source package: `3d_package:87738412-3acd-45d1-bff4-3ab67093470e`. Low-resolution GLB derivative.
- `smithsonian-acropora-palmata.glb` — *Acropora palmata*, Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History / Smithsonian Open Access, CC0. Source package: `3d_package:6fd30c79-3345-4d06-b27c-99d5b320ce5f`. Low-resolution GLB derivative.

For web delivery, the bundled copies keep the original geometry, normal maps, occlusion maps, and color textures while reducing background-detail mesh density and resizing textures from 2048px to 1024px. The resulting set is 7.1 MB instead of 18 MB.

## Abyssal geology

- `polyhaven/rock_07` — **Rock 07**, Poly Haven, CC0.
- `polyhaven/rock_09` — **Rock 09**, Poly Haven, CC0.
- `polyhaven/moon_rock_02` — **Moon Rock 02**, Poly Haven, CC0.

The scanned meshes and PBR texture sets were obtained from Poly Haven's official 1K glTF packages. Their web copies retain the source geometry and ARM/normal/albedo workflow while reducing textures to 640px for the deep-background composition. Together they add approximately 1.2 MB. Source pages: `https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_07`, `https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_09`, and `https://polyhaven.com/a/moon_rock_02`.
